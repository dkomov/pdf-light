# Community code of conduct

We want PDF Light to be useful to designers, developers, translators, and people who are new to open source.

Be respectful and specific. Critique the work, explain your reasoning, assume good faith, and make room for different levels of experience. Respect privacy and intellectual property. Harassment, discriminatory attacks, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and repeated personal insults are not welcome.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close a discussion, issue a warning, or restrict participation depending on severity and repetition. Decisions should be proportionate and focus on protecting participants.

Report abusive content through GitHub's reporting tools. For project-specific concerns, contact the maintainer through the contact options on [dkomov's public profile](https://github.com/dkomov); avoid posting private details in a public issue. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and give participants a fair chance to respond when appropriate.
